# Sada vendor manual

Sada's standing requirements for every factory and supplier. Factories confirm in writing that they
have received and will follow this version.

## 1. Contacts and communication
Who to contact for design, technical, quality, orders, and shipping.

## 2. Code of conduct
No forced, bonded, or child labour; freedom of association; legal wages and working hours; safe
buildings and fire safety; no discrimination or harassment; environmental compliance; no
unauthorised subcontracting. Right to audit.

## 3. Samples
Sample types and quantities per round, labelling of samples (style, size, round, date), courier and address.

## 4. Materials and compliance
Use only approved fabrics and trims (`FAB`/`TRM`). Restricted substances (REACH, OEKO-TEX).
Certificates and test reports to supply, and when.

## 5. Labels
Main label, size label, care and fibre content label (wording and ISO 3758 symbols per product), country
of origin, placement, and language(s).

## 6. Packing
Folding method and size, tissue, polybag (size, warning text, recycled content), hangtag placement,
units per inner pack, size/color assortment per carton.

## 7. Carton marking and shipping
Carton size and maximum weight; shipping marks (PO, style, color, size breakdown, quantity, carton
number of total, gross/net weight); packing list format; shipping documents.

## 8. Quality
Inline and pre-shipment inspection, AQL levels, defect classification (critical / major / minor),
measurement tolerance as per tech pack, and what happens on failure.

## 9. Acknowledgement
Factory name, signatory, date, version received.
