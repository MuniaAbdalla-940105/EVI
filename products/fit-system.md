# Fit system (pants)

Every pant in the launch range (COL-001 #1 wide-leg, #2 straight tailored) comes in three fits: **Straight,
Curvy, Full**. The customer picks her body shape, gets her fit, then picks her size. Shape and size are
separate: every fit comes in XS to XXXL.

Visual version: [EVI Fit System](https://claude.ai/artifact/JZP4SFGupvzf3NaR7EyRHu) (private to the founder).

## Shape to fit

| Body shape | What it means | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hourglass | Bust and hips about the same; waist clearly smaller | Curvy |
| Pear | Hips clearly wider than the bust; defined waist | Curvy |
| Apple | Fuller around the middle; waist close to or wider than bust and hips | Full |
| Rectangle | Bust, waist, and hips about the same | Straight |
| Inverted triangle | Bust or shoulders clearly wider than the hips | Straight |

## The three fits

| Feature | Straight | Curvy | Full |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rise | Mid-high | High | Higher at the front |
| Waist compared with hip | Standard | About one size smaller | Standard |
| Waistband | Flat front, hidden back elastic | Contoured (curved) so the back doesn't gap | Wide and soft, elastic at the back and sides; doesn't dig in or roll |
| Hip, seat, thigh | Standard | Extra room | Standard, a little more thigh room |
| Tummy-smoothing inner panel | No | Sizes L to XXXL | All sizes |
| Leg | Same in all three fits | Same | Same |

The leg is the same in all three fits, so one leg pattern is graded and only the top block (waist, rise,
hip) changes. This keeps pattern and sampling cost down.

## Shape finder rules

The website asks for bust, waist, and hips in cm. These are common rules of thumb, not EVI data, and will be
tuned after fit rounds.

1. Waist ÷ larger of bust and hips ≥ 0.90: **apple** if waist ≥ hips, otherwise **rectangle**.
2. Bust more than 8% larger than hips: **inverted triangle**.
3. Hips more than 8% larger than bust: **pear**.
4. Otherwise, waist ratio ≤ 0.80: **hourglass**; above 0.80: **rectangle**.

Example: bust 112, waist 92, hips 118 → hourglass → Curvy fit.

## Why the Curvy fit exists

The founder's own fit problem (hourglass, larger size): with ready-made pants the waist is too big, the hips
are too tight, and the tummy has no support. The Curvy fit with the inner panel is built to solve this, and it
is the first fit block to sample.
