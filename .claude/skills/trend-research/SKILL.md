# Trend research

Your built-in knowledge is out of date for trends. Everything here comes from web search done now.

1. Scope `$ARGUMENTS`: category, season, region, audience (from personas).
2. Search widely and recently (prefer the last 6 months): trade press, runway and street-style
   coverage, retailer "new in" and bestseller pages, platform reports (search and social trend
   reports), design-system and UX publications, fibre and mill news. Note paid forecasting services
   (e.g. WGSN) as unavailable unless the founder supplies extracts.
3. For each trend: name, description, evidence (≥ 2 independent sources ideally), first seen,
   momentum (emerging / peaking / declining), fit with Sada's brand and personas (high / medium / low),
   and how to apply it (or why to skip it).
4. Save to `docs/01-market/trends/YYYY-MM-<slug>.md` with front matter (`title`, `status: draft`,
   `updated`, `scope`) and a sources table (title, URL, publisher, date, access date).
5. Promote decision-relevant findings to `docs/01-market/insights.md` as `MI-###`.
6. Summarise the top 5 trends to act on and the top 3 to deliberately ignore.
