# Pricing: Egypt launch

## Strategy

**Affordable premium.** EVI prices sit above local Egyptian modest brands (better fit, fabric, and finish)
and below international brands such as Oysho and Zara. The founder set maximum prices in Kuwaiti dinars
(KD); they are converted to Egyptian pounds (EGP) and checked against the Egyptian market.

## Targets

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Launch market | Egypt (Gulf later) |
| Currency | EGP; retail prices include 14% VAT |
| Exchange rate used | 1 KD ≈ EGP 170 (mid-October 2026; check again before launch) |
| Target gross margin | 65% (Assumption: common for direct-to-consumer apparel; confirm in the financial model) |
| Minimum gross margin | 55% (Assumption) |

## Market benchmarks (Egypt, checked October 2026)

| Brand | Item | Price (EGP) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oysho | Wide-leg trousers | 2,999–4,499 |
| Oysho | Leggings | 1,899–2,999 |
| Zara | Women's trousers | Mostly 1,690–2,590; some 3,590–4,290 |
| Local modest brands (Tema, Jamila, Rizq, Wide Leg Egypt) | Tops, pants, sets | About 375–1,850 |

## Price list (proposed)

| Item | Founder's maximum | Maximum in EGP | Proposed EGP price | Position | Target landed cost (65% margin) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic (long tee, base layer) | 7 KD | ≈ 1,190 | **850** | Above local basics, well below Zara | ≤ EGP 260 |
| Top (long shirt, tunic) | 10 KD | ≈ 1,700 | **1,350** | Mid-market | ≤ EGP 415 |
| Pants | 15 KD | ≈ 2,550 | **2,200** | Around Zara, below Oysho | ≤ EGP 675 |
| Hoodie | 15 KD | ≈ 2,550 | **2,200** | Around Zara | ≤ EGP 675 |
| Jacket or blazer | Not set yet | — | **2,900** (proposal, about 17 KD) | Below Oysho and Zara tailoring | ≤ EGP 890 |

How the target cost is calculated: price ÷ 1.14 (remove VAT) × 0.35 (keep 65%). Landed cost means fabric,
trims, sewing, packaging, and transport to the warehouse.

## Notes

- The founder's KD maximums are not too high for Egypt: they sit below Oysho and close to Zara. The proposed
  prices are 10–30% under each maximum to leave room for launch offers without discounting the brand.
- Whether Egyptian factories can hit the target costs at small quantities is the key question for the first
  factory quotes (RFQ).
- Gulf prices will be set separately when the Gulf launch is planned (MI-010).
