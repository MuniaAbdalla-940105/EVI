# Vision and mission

## The name

**Working name: EVI**, short for **EVeryday Item**: the plain, well-made basic every woman reaches for
first. Pending trademark, domain, and social handle checks (see `brand/name-check.md`).
The original name, Sada (سادة, "plain"), was dropped because a Kuwaiti basics brand already uses it.

## Vision

Every woman in the MENA region can find the basics she needs, in a cut made for her body and
her modesty, without searching, compromising, or altering.

## Mission

Make modest everyday basics that fit real MENA bodies, are easy to care for, and quietly look
elegant, and make finding the right fit online as simple as answering three questions.

## The idea in one line

The calm, premium basics of Oysho and Alo, designed modest from the start, and fitted by body shape.

## Problem

Founder's own experience. This is a hypothesis until customer research confirms it.

- Clothes are cut for one body shape. An hourglass, curvy woman buying pants gets a waist that is too big,
  hips that are too tight, and no support at the tummy.
- Larger sizes are hard to find, or are the same pattern scaled up, so they fit badly.
- Tops are often too short or too sheer for modest dressing, so women layer or alter them.
- Basics that look elegant often crease, need ironing, or need special care. That doesn't suit busy
  women and mothers.
- Finding "a formal black pant" or "a long gym top" means searching many stores.

## Who it is for

- **Now:** women, from young adults to millennials (exact age range to confirm), who dress modestly or
  prefer covered, comfortable clothing.
- **Later:** a men's line.
- Every body shape and a wide size range, including plus sizes, as part of the core range rather than
  an extension.

## What Sada sells

All the basics a woman needs, in collections released in colour drops that mix and match with
everything already in the range.

| Use | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Casual | Long tees, oversized shirts, wide-leg and straight pants, leggings |
| Semi-casual | Shirt dresses, tunics, relaxed tailoring |
| Formal | Tailored black pants, blouses, long blazers |
| Active | Gym leggings, long active tops |
| Later | Hijabs, a men's line |

Not now: footwear, bags, accessories.

## How customers shop

1. **Body shape:** choose your shape (e.g. hourglass, pear, apple, rectangle, inverted triangle).
2. **Occasion:** what you're wearing it for (casual, semi-casual, formal, active).
3. **Style and fit:** e.g. oversized shirt with leggings, or oversized shirt with wide pants.
4. **Size:** a size chart in body measurements, with fit notes for each shape.
5. **Mix and match:** outfits built from pieces across drops, in colours that work together.

## Product principles

1. **Fit by body shape.** Key styles, starting with pants, come in cuts for different shapes, with
   rise, waist, hip, and tummy shaped to match. Sizes cover the full range, including plus sizes.
2. **Modest by design.** Tops long enough to cover the hips, opaque fabrics, and loose-enough cuts.
3. **Wash and wear.** Fabrics that resist creasing, need little or no ironing, and survive frequent washing.
4. **Quiet elegance.** Plain pieces with one small, refined detail each: a seam, a cuff, a button, a drape.
   Feminine, with an edge.
5. **Colours that combine.** Each drop adds colours that work with the core palette.
6. **Tested before sold.** Every product is worn and tested by the founder; only approved products go online.

## Values

1. **Simple (sada).** Nothing unnecessary in the design, the shopping, or the care.
2. **Made for real bodies.** Every shape and size is planned from the start.
3. **Honest.** Every claim about fabric and fit is backed by a test or a sample.
4. **Calm confidence.** Elegant without being loud.

## Channels

Online first (own website). A physical store may follow once online sales prove demand.

## What Sada is not

- Not fast fashion or trend-chasing. Basics stay in the range; colours refresh.
- Not heavily embellished. One refined detail, never many.
- Not one-size-fits-all patterns stretched to larger sizes.
- Not footwear or accessories, for now.
