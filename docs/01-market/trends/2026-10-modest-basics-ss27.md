# Trends: modest everyday basics, 2026 and SS27

Researched 2026-10-24. Most sources are trend services, retailer blogs, and trade-fair notes rather than
paid forecasters (WGSN and similar were not available). "Momentum" is a judgement from how many sources agree.

| Trend | What it is | Evidence | Momentum | Fit with EVI | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet simplicity | Less embellishment; cleaner shapes; monochrome outfits; buying fewer, better pieces | Gulf trend pieces | Peaking | High | EVI's core: plain pieces, one refined detail |
| Relaxed tailoring | Wide-leg trousers, oversized shirts, longline blazers, softly structured outerwear; moving away from shapeless oversize | 2026 modest trend reports; SS27 forecasts | Peaking | High | Launch range: wide-leg and straight pants, long shirts, longline blazers, soft jackets |
| Functional fabrics | Wrinkle-free, breathable, temperature-regulating fabrics | 2026 modest trend reports | Rising | High | Supports "wash and wear"; specify crease resistance and breathability in fabric tests |
| Natural, breathable fibres | Cotton-modal blends, linen, organic cotton, French terry, textured weaves (slub, seersucker) | SS27 fabric forecasts | Rising | High | Fabric story for summer pieces; check opacity in light colours |
| Earthy neutrals | Sand, cream, ivory, olive, soft brown, clay, ochre | Gulf and SS27 colour forecasts | Steady | High | Core palette that mixes and matches across drops |
| Accent colours | Cobalt or luminous blue; dusty pink, lavender, sage; Saudi coverage mentions pastels | SS27 colour forecasts; Saudi trend piece | Emerging | Medium | One accent colour per drop, never the core |
| Matching sets | Two-piece sets in the same fabric | GCC retailer trend piece | Steady | Medium | Sell top and pant in the same fabric, worn together or apart |
| Longevity | "Slow fashion": clothes made to last | Trend articles | Rising | High | Message: basics that last; avoid fast-fashion markdown habits |

## Trends to skip

- Heavy embellishment and statement prints: against the brand.
- Excessively oversized, shapeless silhouettes: forecasters report a move away from them, and they don't flatter all body shapes.
- Fast colour churn: keep a stable core palette.
