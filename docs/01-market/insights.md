# Market insights

| ID | Insight | Evidence | Confidence | Implication for EVI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MI-001 | Modest fashion is a large, growing global market, and Saudi Arabia is one of its three biggest markets | US$347bn in 2024, 5.1% a year to 2029 (SGIE 2025/26) | High | The category is worth building for; Saudi Arabia is a priority market |
| MI-002 | Online is a large and rising share of fashion spending in Saudi Arabia | 15–24% online in 2025, modelled 30–39% by 2031 (Ken Research, ECDB) | Medium | Online-first launch is viable in the Gulf |
| MI-003 | Larger sizes are the norm, not the exception, among women in EVI's markets | Female obesity about 48–59% in Egypt, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait (World Obesity Atlas, to verify); 41–44% in Gulf states (WHO 2016) | Medium | XS–XXXL must be core; size up to XXXL from launch, and consider 4XL later |
| MI-004 | Plus-size modest clothing is poorly served; scaled-up patterns don't fit | Designer interview (Hyphen, 2026); few plus-size modest brands found | Low to medium | Body-shape fits and properly graded patterns are a real differentiator |
| MI-005 | Global leisure brands (Oysho, Alo) are present through franchise operators but are not designed modest | Alshaya and Azadea/Alhokair announcements; brand ranges | Medium | "Modest by design, the calm of Oysho and Alo" is an open position |
| MI-006 | Local Egyptian modest basics sell at roughly EGP 375–1,650 per piece, with cash on delivery | Tema, Jamila, Rizq sites and 2025 guides | Medium | In Egypt, EVI's mid-premium price must be clearly justified (fit, fabric, design) |
| MI-007 | Payment preferences differ: COD still large in Egypt; buy-now-pay-later widely known in Saudi Arabia | COD about 34% of payment value in Egypt (2025); 75% of Saudis know BNPL, 33% used it (Visa 2024) | Medium | Launch with COD in Egypt and BNPL in the Gulf |
| MI-008 | Egypt has a growing garment manufacturing base | Exports about US$3.4bn in 2025 (+20%), 1,266 exporters | High | Produce in Egypt, close to the company and with short shipping to the Gulf |
| MI-009 | Social platforms drive apparel discovery in Saudi Arabia | Snapchat-commissioned study, 2025 (sponsored) | Low to medium | Plan content for Snapchat, Instagram, TikTok; test try-on features later |
| MI-010 | Price anchors for basics: Oysho Saudi Arabia SAR 199–389 per bottom; FARES US$24–34 per layer and about US$58 per pant; Tema (Egypt) EGP 425–1,850 per piece | Teardowns in `competitors/` (2026-10-24) | Medium | Gulf and Egypt need different price points; set prices per market channel |
| MI-011 | Current trends match EVI's concept: quiet simplicity, relaxed tailoring, wrinkle-free and breathable fabrics, earthy neutrals | `trends/2026-10-modest-basics-ss27.md` | Medium | Launch range and fabric story can follow trends without chasing them |
| MI-012 | The closest concept (FARES) is creator-led and North American; regional modest brands lead with dresses or low prices | Teardowns | Medium | Being regional, fit-led, and size-inclusive is EVI's clearest difference |
