## What we learn from them

- The calm, soft-neutral look the founder likes works in the region at **SAR 199–389 per bottom**: a useful price anchor for EVI in Saudi Arabia.
- EVI's opening: the same calm style, but modest by design (longer tops, opaque fabrics, looser cuts) and with real plus sizes.
