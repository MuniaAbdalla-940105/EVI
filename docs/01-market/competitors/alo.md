## What we learn from them

- Gulf customers pay premium prices for calm, wellness-led basics.
- Alo stops at XL and isn't modest: EVI can offer the same feeling to women Alo doesn't dress.
- EVI's gym drop (drop 2) will compete here directly; quality of fabric (no pilling, opaque) must match.
