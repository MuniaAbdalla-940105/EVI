## What we learn from them

- The "modest capsule basics" idea already works for a creator-led brand: the concept is validated.
- **Price anchors:** basic layers about US$24–34, pants about US$58.
- EVI's difference must be fit (body shapes, XS–XXXL) and regional focus (Arabic, Gulf and Egypt delivery, local payments).
