# Market analysis: modest everyday basics in the Gulf and Egypt

First pass, from desk research (published reports, press, brand sites). Market-report figures vary widely
between publishers because they define the market differently; ranges are shown instead of single numbers.
Every figure is listed in `sources.md`. Competitor teardowns are in `competitors/`; the trend report is in
`trends/2026-10-modest-basics-ss27.md`.

## Market definition

| Dimension | EVI's market |
| --- | --- |
| Product | Women's everyday basics designed modest: tops, pants, jackets, blazers. Activewear from drop 2 |
| Customer | Gen Alpha (teens) to Millennials; XS to XXXL; every body shape |

## Competitive landscape

See `competitor-matrix.md`. In summary:

- **Global leisure and athleisure brands** (Oysho, Alo) are in the Gulf and Egypt through big franchise
  operators, but they are not designed modest and use standard size ranges.
- **Marketplaces** (Namshi, Ounass, Noon) run "modest" edits that mix many brands; none owns the
  everyday-basics position.
- **Regional modest brands** focus on dresses, abayas, sets, and occasion pieces (Saudi) or on affordable
  hijab-friendly basics (Egypt, roughly EGP 375–1,650 per piece).
- **Plus-size modest** is served by a few designers (e.g. The Modist in Dubai) and by non-modest plus-size
  brands entering the region.

No brand found combines all five of EVI's planned features: body-shape fits, XS–XXXL, a mix-and-match
basics system, a calm mid-premium look, and modest-by-design cuts. This is a desk-research finding, not proof
that the gap is empty.

## Supply side

Egypt's ready-made garment exports grew about 20% to about US$3.4bn in 2025, with 1,266 exporting companies.
EVI can source and produce close to its home base, with short shipping to the Gulf. Minimum order quantities
for small brands are not known yet.

## Opportunities and risks

| Opportunities | Risks |
| --- | --- |
| A clear gap: modest-by-design basics fitted by body shape, in a full size range | Mid-premium prices are well above local Egyptian modest basics; the value must be obvious |
| Online fashion is a large and growing share of spending in Saudi Arabia and the UAE | Body-shape fits multiply stock-keeping units; with a minimum budget this limits the first range |
| Local manufacturing in Egypt | Big franchise operators control mall retail; EVI competes online only |
| Payment options (COD in Egypt, BNPL in the Gulf) are well established | Several currencies, cross-border shipping, and returns add cost and complexity |
| | Selling to Gen Alpha (under 18) brings data-protection and marketing rules |
