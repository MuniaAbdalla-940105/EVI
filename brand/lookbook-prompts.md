# Lookbook image prompts

Prompts for an AI image generator (for example ChatGPT, Gemini, or Midjourney) to see the launch range on a
realistic model before samples exist. Generated images are for internal review only: they are concept
images, not product photos, and must not be used to sell or advertise products.

## Base prompt (use with every look)

> Photorealistic fashion e-commerce photo, full body, one woman in her late twenties with a Middle Eastern
> appearance, hair visible and styled simply, natural makeup. Modern city basics brand, calm and premium,
> in the style of Oysho and COS. Modest by cut: tops cover the hips, opaque fabrics, relaxed but not
> shapeless. Plain garments without logos or prints. Soft daylight, minimal sand-coloured stone wall and
> pale floor, relaxed natural pose. No headscarf, no traditional or religious styling, no jewellery
> except small gold earrings.

Colours: Black, Sand (warm beige), Ivory (soft off-white), Sage (muted grey-green).
