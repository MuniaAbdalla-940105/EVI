# Lookbook image prompts

Prompts for an AI image generator (for example ChatGPT, Gemini, or Midjourney) to see the launch range on a
realistic model before samples exist. Generated images are for internal review only: they are concept
images, not product photos, and must not be used to sell or advertise products.

## Base prompt (use with every look)

> Photorealistic fashion e-commerce photo, full body, one woman in her late twenties with a Middle Eastern
> appearance, hair visible and styled simply, natural makeup. Modern city basics brand, calm and premium,
> in the style of Oysho and COS. Modest by cut: tops cover the hips, opaque fabrics, relaxed but not
> shapeless. Plain garments without logos or prints. Soft daylight, minimal sand-coloured stone wall and
> pale floor, relaxed natural pose. No headscarf, no traditional or religious styling, no jewellery
> except small gold earrings.

Colours: Black, Charcoal (warm dark grey), Sand (warm beige), Ivory (soft off-white), Sage (muted grey-green).

## Sketch style for ChatGPT (day looks)

Watercolour fashion illustrations like a designer's approval book. Paste one prompt per message, one image at a
time, portrait format. Each prompt is complete on its own.

**Tips**
- After the first good image, start the next prompt with: "Same illustration style, paper, and layout as the
  previous image."
- If something comes out wrong, reply with one fix only, e.g. "Make the trousers wide-leg and floor length" or
  "Cover the legs fully; no bare skin below the knee".
- Ask for "portrait 2:3" if the image comes out square.

**Sunday, office**
> Fashion design sketch in watercolour and fine ink line on textured white paper, like a page in a designer's
> approval book. One woman, full body, standing in a relaxed pose, with a soft, light city street sketched
> behind her. She has a curvy hourglass figure, plus size, light olive skin, dark hair in a sleek low bun.
> She wears a warm Charcoal grey suit: straight tailored trousers to the ankle with a pressed crease and a
> longline single-button blazer to mid-thigh; underneath, an Ivory fitted long-sleeve mock-neck top. Black
> leather loafers, brown leather tote. Modern, calm, premium city basics in the style of COS and Oysho;
> modest by cut, no headscarf, no jewellery except small gold earrings. To the right, two pinned fabric
> swatches with pinked edges and small handwritten labels: "Charcoal suiting" and "Ivory rib". Header text
> at the top: "EVI Everyday · Sunday". Portrait 2:3.

### Review of the first round (2026-10-28)

All seven looks came out on brand: modern, calm, modest by cut, correct colours and pieces. Fixes for the next round:

| Look | Fix | Reply to ChatGPT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sunday | Blazer has two buttons; the range has one. The Charcoal swatch reads black | "Make the blazer single-button, and the Charcoal suiting swatch a warm dark grey, lighter than black" |
| All | Most figures came out slim and similar, so the fit-by-body-shape promise doesn't show | Add "clearly visible [shape] figure, UK size [16/20]" and "realistic proportions, not a fashion-model body" |
| Wednesday, Sunday | Straight pants look tapered and cropped | "Straight leg, same width from knee to hem, ankle length" |
| Monday | Tee is tucked in | Fine for a fitted tee under an open shirt; keep |
